The Art of the Build

At Nidal Spec, we believe that every car has a story to tell. Our warehouse is a special place where we take your ideas and turn them into reality. Whether you have a classic car that needs a new life or a modern car that you want to make faster, we are here to help.

We focus on the small details that make a big difference. This includes changing how your car looks with custom bodywork, making the engine run better, and ensuring every part works perfectly. We treat every car as if it were our own, using the best tools and taking our time to get the job done right.

Working with us is easy and honest. We love what we do, and we want you to love your car even more than you did before. We do not just fix vehicles; we reinvent them so they are ready for the road. Every project starts with your vision and ends with a masterpiece that you will be proud to drive every day.
